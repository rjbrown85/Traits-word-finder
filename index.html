Start with the character's clues.

What does the character say, do, think, or feel? Gather more than one clue before deciding on a trait.

Check the meaning.

Content means quietly satisfied. Excited adds energy or anticipation. Both belong near happy, but they do different jobs.

Check the intensity.

Annoyed is usually milder than angry. Furious is much stronger. These are useful comparisons, not exact scores.

Compare the shades of meaning.

Generous emphasizes giving. Compassionate emphasizes caring about someone who is struggling. Neither is always a stronger version of kind.

Keep feelings and traits distinct.

A character can feel scared and act courageously. Being quiet does not automatically mean being shy. One mistake does not prove that someone is careless.
